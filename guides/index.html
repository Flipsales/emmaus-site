Guides for reading the Bible and learning to pray

Plain, Scripture-first answers to the questions people ask when they are starting out. Read them in any order.

Start reading

New to the Bible, or coming back to it. Begin here.

  • Where to start reading the Bible

    Not sure where to start reading the Bible? Begin with a Gospel like John or Mark, or with Proverbs. Here is how to choose, plus a simple first week.

  • How to read the Bible for beginners

    How to read the Bible as a beginner: pray first, read one chapter slowly, write down one thing and one question, then come back tomorrow. A gentle guide.

  • A Bible reading plan for beginners: one chapter a day

    A Bible reading plan you can't fall behind on: one chapter a day, Proverbs first, then the New Testament, then the rest. No deadlines and no streaks.

  • A simple Advent Bible reading plan for 2026

    A simple Advent Bible reading plan for 2026: one short passage a day from November 29 to Christmas Eve. No streaks, no guilt, and no catching up.

Understand what you read

Simple ways to slow down and see what a passage is saying.

  • How to read the Bible and understand it

    To understand the Bible, read whole passages in context, ask who wrote it and to whom, let Scripture explain Scripture, and pray. Simple steps for any reader.

  • How to study the Bible for beginners

    A simple way to study the Bible as a beginner: pick one book, read a short passage, and use SOAP (Scripture, Observation, Application, Prayer).

  • How to make Bible reading a habit

    To make Bible reading a habit, keep it small, read at the same time and place each day, tie it to something you already do, and restart without guilt.

Prayer and quiet time

Talking with God, and making room for it each day.

  • How to pray

    How to pray: talk to God honestly, in your own words. Use the Lord's Prayer as a pattern: praise, ask, confess and trust. A gentle guide for beginners.

  • How to have a quiet time with God

    A quiet time is a few minutes alone with God each day. Find a quiet place, pray, read a short passage, reflect, and pray again. Here is a simple guide.

Passages explained

A closer look at passages people ask about most.

  • What happened on the road to Emmaus (Luke 24)

    On the road to Emmaus, the risen Jesus walked with two disciples, opened the Scriptures to them, and was recognized when He broke bread (Luke 24:13-35).

  • What John 3:16 means

    John 3:16 means God loves the world so much that He gave His only Son, so that everyone who believes in Him will not perish but have eternal life.
