About Emmaus: Walk with God

Emmaus: Walk with God is a daily discipleship app for iPhone and iPad, published by Disciple Maker, LLC. It includes the whole Bible and is built around what happens around your reading: a morning word, prayer for the people you love, an evening look back at your day, and a friend to walk with.

Scripture and daily rhythms

Ask about a verse and be pointed back to the passage. Read the whole Bible one chapter a day, beginning with Proverbs, then the New Testament, then everything else. Build daily rhythms of prayer and reflection, and invite a friend to walk with you.

Emmaus is a tool. The Bible is the source. The Holy Spirit is the teacher.

Looking for a place to begin? Start with Scripture and a simple prayer.
