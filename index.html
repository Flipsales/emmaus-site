Understand what you read

Stop feeling lost in Scripture.

Ever read a verse and wondered what it means? Ask. Emmaus explains who’s speaking, what it meant then, and why it matters now — in plain words — and points you back to the passage so you can see it for yourself. Type it, or tap the orb and just talk.

  • Explain a verse, pray with me, talk through what’s ahead — one tap to start.
  • Talk it through out loud and hear the answer read back to you, calm and unhurried. It never scolds, never scores, never shames.
  • In hands-free conversation your speech is recognized right on your iPhone when the device supports it; otherwise a short recording is transcribed once, used once, and never kept.
  • It can get things wrong — so it always sends you back to the Bible itself.

A word just for you

Your life isn’t generic. Your daily encouragement shouldn’t be either.

Each morning Emmaus writes a short devotional for you, grounded in Scripture and shaped by your walk — the people you’re praying for, what you’re wrestling with, where you are in the Word. Tell it once; it remembers, and brings it back at the right moment. An app that asks how the conversation with your brother went is a different kind of app.

  • Everything it remembers is visible to you — pin what matters, forget anything, or set it straight just by telling it.
  • Save the words that meet you, and listen to them read aloud.
  • Your memories are never shared, sold, or used to advertise to you.

  • A prayer list that celebrates answered prayer, and Scripture memory that helps the Word stay with you.

The proper place of Emmaus

Keep returning to the Source.

Emmaus is a tool.
The Bible is the source.
The Holy Spirit is the teacher.

Emmaus was created to help you build rhythms of Scripture, prayer, service, reflection, and daily inventory—but it should never replace the Bible or the leading of the Holy Spirit. Translations, denominations, pastors, and teachers may interpret passages differently, and Emmaus can get things wrong. Read and engage with Scripture for yourself—including with a physical Bible when possible—verify what you learn, and invite the Holy Spirit to guide you.

Our goal is not for people to become dependent on Emmaus. Our goal is for Emmaus to help people become more dependent on God.
