For the 11pm heart

Someone to say it to.

Some things you can’t type. Tap the orb and just talk — about the fear, the verse, the whole day. Emmaus listens, answers out loud in a calm and unhurried way, and points you gently back to Scripture. It never scolds, never scores, never shames.

  • In hands-free conversation your speech is recognized right on your iPhone when the device supports it; otherwise a short recording is transcribed once, used once, and never kept.
  • The evening examen tucks the day in: three unhurried breaths, honest reflection, grace.
  • It reads Scripture aloud — for the commute, the dishes, the night you can’t sleep.
  • Talking to Emmaus is free for everyone — its spoken replies are part of Premium.

The Emmaus Journey

Never “behind” again.

We don’t have Bible plans. The plan is the Bible. Emmaus walks you through all 1,189 chapters — Proverbs first, then the New Testament, then everything else — one chapter at a time. Today’s chapter is simply the next one. Miss a day, miss a month: the road waits for you.

  • A few unhurried minutes a day. Roughly three years for the whole Bible. It’s a walk, not a race.
  • Your walk through the Word has no deadline, no streak, and no “missed day.” On purpose. Forever.
  • Ask the companion about anything you just read — a word, a verse, a wrestle.

Walking partners

Not alone on the road.

Invite one person to walk with you — a friend, a mentor, someone you’re discipling. They see that you walked today, never how far or how fast. They can carry your prayer requests and send you a word of courage when you need it most.

  • No feed. No followers. No strangers. Every connection is invited, mutual, and endable by either of you, anytime.
  • Partners you share with see your prayers, rhythms, and goals — never your journal, your conversations, or how far you are in the Word.

Your companion

It remembers your walk.

Tell it once. Emmaus remembers your people, your prayers, what you’re wrestling with, and where you left off — and brings it back at exactly the right moment. An app that asks how the conversation with your brother went is a different kind of app.

  • Everything it remembers is visible to you — pin what matters, forget anything, or set it straight just by telling it.
  • Your memories are never shared, sold, or used to advertise to you.

Why it feels different
