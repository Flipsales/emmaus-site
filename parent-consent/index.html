Review before you decide

Nothing is opened for the child unless you give permission and submit the signed form.

Who operates Emmaus: Disciple Maker LLC, 126 Hammocks Court, Green Acres, Florida 33413. Contact support@getemmaus.app.

Before approval: Emmaus keeps only the random permission request, the parent email entered here, and short-lived protected network-security data used to stop pairing-code abuse. It does not ask the child for an account email, name, prayer, journal, voice, coach message, or other account content.

Core information: If approved, Emmaus stores the child’s first name, parent-managed account email, Bible reading activity, goals, app settings, prayers, and journals. Supabase hosts the account and stored content. We use that information to provide, secure, and support the account. Product metrics are aggregated or de-identified where feasible. We do not sell child information or use it for advertising.

Optional AI: Only if you choose it, prompts and relevant Emmaus context may be sent to Anthropic and OpenAI for coaching, devotional generation, safety screening, and memory. User API content is not used to train their general models under their business/API terms. Emmaus stores coach conversations until the account holder deletes them or deletes the account.

Optional voice: Only if you choose both AI and voice, recordings are sent to OpenAI for transcription and text may be sent for generated speech. Emmaus does not need voice to use the typed experience.

Optional connections: Only if you choose it, the child can connect with an invited walking partner. Partners never see journals or coach chats. They can see only the limited walk information and prayers the child explicitly marks for partners, and can exchange encouragement notes.

Other providers: Resend delivers necessary parent/account email. RevenueCat and Apple process subscriptions and purchases. Apple may provide a coarse age range. Calendar event details remain on the device and are not inserted into AI prompts.

Your control: You can say no to AI, voice, and connections while allowing the core Bible activities. From this private link you can withdraw permission, ask to review/correct the child’s information, or permanently delete the account. We retain child information only while needed to provide the account and meet security/legal obligations, then securely delete it under our retention schedule.
