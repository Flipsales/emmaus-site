Delete your Emmaus account

Emmaus: Walk with God, by Disciple Maker LLC

You can delete your Emmaus account and everything in it at any time. You don't need to give a reason.

Option 1: In the app

Open Emmaus and go to Settings › Your data › Delete account. Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: By email, without the app

If you no longer have the app, email support@getemmaus.app with the subject “Delete my Emmaus account”.

  • Send it from the email address you use to sign in, so we know the request is yours.
  • If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, or you can't send from that address, tell us. We'll write back to confirm the account is yours before we delete anything.

We'll delete the account and email you when it's done, within 30 days of your request and usually much sooner.

Your subscription is separate. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it where you bought it: in your Apple Account (Subscriptions) on iPhone, or in the Google Play Store › Subscriptions on Android.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account, including:

  • your profile, sign-in, and email preferences
  • journals, examens, prayer requests, goals, notes, memory verses, and habit history
  • conversations with the AI and everything it remembers about you
  • your walking-partner connections, the invitations you sent, and the encouragements you sent and received (the other person loses these too)
  • your devices' notification tokens

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies can stay in encrypted backups for up to 7 days, then they're purged. Backups are only for disaster recovery.
  • Safety logs: records of which safety rule applied in a conversation, with no copy of what was said, are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Parent consent records (only for accounts created with a parent's permission): the narrow record of that permission is kept for up to 3 years to show it was given, then deleted.
  • We keep anything else only where the law requires it.

Full details are in section 3 of our Privacy Policy.

Delete some data without closing your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove things. In the app you can delete any journal entry, prayer, or conversation, and under Settings › Your data › What Emmaus remembers you can forget any memory. If you'd like us to do it for you, email support@getemmaus.app.
