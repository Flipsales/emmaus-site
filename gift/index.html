Gift terms and refunds

A gift is a one-time Apple purchase for one other person's Emmaus account. The price shown by Apple before purchase applies. It provides one calendar year from the recipient's explicit claim, with no automatic renewal. Unclaimed gifts do not expire; a claimed gift cannot be reassigned or added to existing paid or gifted access.

Apple handles payment and decides refund eligibility. The purchaser can request a refund through Apple. If Apple refunds a gift, it can no longer be claimed and access already granted by that gift ends. Independent Apple subscriptions or other account access are not cancelled by a gift refund.
